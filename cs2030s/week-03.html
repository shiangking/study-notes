CS2030S Guide: Polymorphism, Substitution, and Abstraction

Primary source: Week 3 lecture notes, Units 13–18. This guide covers Liskov Substitution, Polymorphism, Dynamic Binding, Abstract Classes, and Interfaces.

1. The central idea

Polymorphism allows code to be written in terms of higher-level abstractions rather than concrete implementations, enabling open-ended extensibility without modifying existing code.
